Concrete & Construction Services

Specialty concrete and construction services engineered for structural performance across the Bay Area.

What We Do

Specialists, Not Generalists

Every service we offer is backed by dedicated equipment, trained crews, and decades of field experience. We focus on what we do best — structural concrete, excavation, and drilling — so your project gets the specialized attention it deserves.

01

Excavation

Precision excavation in soft, medium, and rocky soils with depths up to 6 meters and slope control. Mechanical and manual equipment for restricted spaces.

Technical Specifications

  • Excavation capacity in soft, medium, and rocky soils
  • Depths of up to 6 meters with slope control and shoring
  • Mechanical and manual excavation equipment for restricted spaces
Learn More About Excavation
02

Concrete Pouring

Structural concrete pours for slabs, columns, beams, and walls, from f'c 3,000 to 6,000 PSI per ACI standards. Mix control, vibration, and curing at every stage.

Technical Specifications

  • Pouring of slabs, footings, columns, beams, walls, and other structural elements.
  • Structural concrete ranging from 3,000 to 6,000 PSI or as specified by project requirements.
  • Mix control, placement, vibration, and curing performed in accordance with ACI standards.
Learn More About Concrete Pouring
03

Core Drilling

Diamond core drilling from ½" to 24" in reinforced concrete, rock, and masonry. Clean, vibration-free cuts for installations and precision anchors.

Technical Specifications

  • Drilling diameters from ½" to 24"
  • Capacity to drill through reinforced concrete, rock, and masonry
  • Diamond core systems for clean, vibration-free cuts
Learn More About Core Drilling
04

Portable Drilling

Compact equipment brought directly to your site, ideal for tight spaces, heights, and hard-to-reach locations. Horizontal, vertical, and angled drilling for MEP installations.

Technical Specifications

  • Compact equipment operable in spaces less than 1 meter wide
  • Horizontal, vertical, and angled drilling as required
  • Ideal for MEP installations, anchors, and pipe sleeves in finished structures
05

Demolition

Selective, partial, or total demolition with prior structural planning. Hydraulic breakers, floor saws, and diamond cutting, with debris disposal per environmental regulations.

Technical Specifications

  • Selective, partial, or total demolition with prior structural planning
  • Use of hydraulic breakers, floor saws, and diamond wire cutting
  • Debris management and disposal in accordance with environmental regulations
06

Sidewalks

Construction and repair of sidewalks with minimum f'c 3,000 PSI concrete. Control joints every 4 to 6 meters with broom, stamped, or smooth finishes.

Technical Specifications

  • Minimum f'c 3,000 PSI concrete with a standard thickness of 4"
  • Control and expansion joints every 4 to 6 linear meters
  • Broom, stamped, or smooth finish as specified by the project
07

Concrete Pumping

Pumping with vertical reach up to 150 meters and capacity up to 90 m³ per hour. Ideal for placement at height, hard-to-access areas, or large volumes on tight schedules.

Technical Specifications

  • Vertical reach of up to 150 meters and horizontal reach of up to 300 meters
  • Pumping capacity of up to 90 m³ per hour
  • Compatible with conventional, pumpable, and specialty concrete mixes
08

Shotcrete

Wet and dry mix shotcrete with strengths from f'c 4,000 to 8,000 PSI. Applied for slope stabilization, tunnels, retaining walls, and structural reinforcement.

Technical Specifications

  • Achievable strengths from f'c 4,000 to f'c 8,000 PSI
  • Wet and dry mix application depending on project conditions
  • Application thickness from 2" to 12" in controlled layers
09

Concrete Finishing

Polished, burnished, broom, stamped, and exposed aggregate finishes per ACI 117 tolerances. Includes hardeners, sealers, and surface treatments.

Technical Specifications

  • Flatness tolerances FF/FL per ACI 117 specifications
  • Finishes: polished, burnished, broom, stamped, and exposed aggregate
  • Application of hardeners, sealers, and surface treatments
10

Shoring

Temporary shoring systems with load capacity up to 20 tons, designed per load analysis and site conditions. Safe support during excavations and demolitions.

Technical Specifications

  • Steel shoring systems with load capacity of up to 20 tons
  • Shoring design based on load analysis and site conditions
  • Compatible with slab, wall, excavation, and repair shoring
11

Waterproofing

Liquid membrane, bituminous sheet, and crystallization systems, applicable under positive and negative pressure. Waterproofing warranty up to 10 years.

Technical Specifications

  • Liquid membrane, bituminous sheet, and crystallization systems
  • Applicable under positive and negative pressure depending on structural conditions
  • Waterproofing warranty of up to 10 years depending on the system applied
12

Retrofits

Structural reinforcement with carbon fiber (CFRP), concrete jacketing, and steel per ASCE 41 and ACI 369 standards. Seismic improvement without major demolition.

Technical Specifications

  • Reinforcement with carbon fiber (CFRP), concrete jacketing, and structural steel
  • Prior structural assessment per ASCE 41 and ACI 369 standards
  • Increased seismic and load capacity without major demolition
